About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Chinese Language and Literature at Hanyang University, working under the supervision of Prof. Sang-Im Lee-Kim. My research focuses on experimental phonetics and laboratory phonology, with a particular interest in the acoustic and articulatory realization of r-suffixation in Beijing Mandarin.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I received my B.A. in Chinese Language and Literature from Ocean University of China and my M.A. in Teaching Chinese to Speakers of Other Languages from Beijing Language and Culture University. I then worked as a Chinese language instructor at the School of International Education, Xi’an Jiaotong University for one year.
